# Film 01

Untitled. The crowd names it last.

Phase 1, the spine, is open. Anyone who joins can send ideas for the
film's logline, cast, places and beat outline at indiecrew.co/adhd. The
project lead picks one for each slot; everyone who sends an idea is
credited as founding crew.

- `spine/spine.json`: the ground rules now, the whole frozen spine once the
  phase wraps up.
- `slots/`: the spine slots. They open in order through `depends_on`.
- `canon/`: each picked idea, credited to its author.

When the spine is wrapped up, the story slots appear in `slots/`.
